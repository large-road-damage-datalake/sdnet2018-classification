# Abstract

SDNET2018 Classification is a curated packaging of the SDNET2018 concrete crack dataset for reproducible model development and benchmarking.

The source dataset contains 56,091 256x256 RGB image patches (~60 mm x 60 mm each, crack widths 0.06–25 mm) segmented from 230 full-resolution (4068x3456) Nikon digital RGB images of concrete bridge decks (54), pavements (104), and walls (72) captured at a 500 mm working distance at Utah State University, labeled as cracked (8,484) or non-cracked (47,607) and released with a peer-reviewed data article.

This package preserves publication metadata and provides generated statistics and visualization artifacts for the Large Road Damage DataLake.
